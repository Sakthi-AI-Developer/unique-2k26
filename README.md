## 📌 About the Symposium

**UNIQUE & ZENTHRA 2K26** is a National Level Technical Symposium designed to bring together students, innovators, developers, and technology enthusiasts to showcase their technical knowledge, creativity, problem-solving skills, and innovation.

The symposium features a combination of **technical and non-technical events**, providing participants with opportunities to compete, collaborate, learn, and experience real-world challenges.

# 🎯 Events

## 💻 Technical Events

### 01. GLITCH VERSE
Challenge your technical thinking and problem-solving skills through an engaging technical competition.

### 02. MIND SPARK
An innovation-focused event where participants explore ideas and solutions around emerging technologies.

### 03. CODE HUNT
A team-based technical challenge where participants solve problems, interact with clues, and race against time.

### 04. VIBE CRAFT
A creative technology challenge where teams build and showcase their own digital portfolio using their preferred software tools.

---

## 🎭 Non-Technical Events

### 01. BGM CHARADES
A fun-filled music and guessing challenge designed to test observation and entertainment skills.

### 02. SHERLOCK HOLMES
Step into the role of a detective, analyze clues and evidence, and uncover the mystery.

### 03. CLUE STROME
A thrilling clue-based challenge that tests logical thinking, observation, and teamwork.
